Features
Everything a busy venue needs,
nothing it doesn't
One system for orders, stock, staff, and financials. No patchwork of apps. No spreadsheets on the side.

Point of Sale

Fast order entry with table management, split bills, discounts, and multi-payment. Designed for speed at peak hours.

Multi-Terminal

Bar, kitchen, and floor staff on separate terminals, all synced in real time. Orders flow instantly to the right station.

Stock Management

Track every bottle, ingredient, and supply. Auto-deduct from orders. Get alerts before you run out. Full purchase order flow.

Financial Wallets

Cash, card, mobile money, credit accounts. Every transaction tracked. Daily reconciliation built in. No money goes missing.

Kitchen Orders

Digital kitchen display replaces paper chits. Orders arrive instantly, marked off as completed. No more lost tickets.

Staff & Security

PIN-based staff login, role-based access, void approvals, and a full audit log. Every action tracked to the person who did it.

Offline-first architecture

Not "works offline sometimes." The entire system is designed to run without a connection. The cloud is a convenience, not a dependency.

Instant sync on reconnect

When connectivity returns, everything syncs automatically. No manual steps, no data loss, no conflicts.

No subscription lock-in

Your data lives on your hardware. No monthly SaaS fees that stop your business when a payment bounces. One-time setup, runs forever.

Multi-currency ready

Handle USD, ZiG, rand, and any local currency. Automatic conversion at daily rates. Built for the reality of African commerce.
